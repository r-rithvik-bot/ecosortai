About EcoSort AI

A student-built prototype for the HCL Jigsaw Innovation Competition, designed to make waste segregation simple, engaging, and rewarding.

The Problem

Most households and schools mix recyclable, organic, and hazardous waste together, making recycling inefficient and increasing landfill pressure. Many people simply don't know which bin an item belongs in, or why it matters.

Incorrect segregation contaminates entire batches of recyclables, meaning materials that could have been reused end up in landfills anyway.

Our Solution

EcoSort AI simulates an AI-powered waste classifier that instantly tells users the correct bin, disposal method, and an interesting environmental fact for any item — turning a confusing decision into a 10-second lookup.

A built-in EcoScore system gamifies the process, rewarding users with points and badges for consistently sorting waste correctly.

Objectives
  • Simplify waste identification for all ages
  • Encourage consistent, correct recycling habits
  • Make sustainability engaging through gamification
  • Provide a scalable base for real AI integration
Benefits
  • Reduces landfill contamination
  • Builds eco-conscious habits early
  • Saves energy, water, and raw materials
  • Makes classrooms and homes greener
Prototype Note

This version simulates AI predictions using a predefined lookup table instead of a trained model, so it runs instantly on any machine with no GPU or dataset required — while keeping the same architecture a real model would use.
